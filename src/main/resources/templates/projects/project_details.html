Project Overview

I designed and developed a robust backend platform for an e-commerce website, providing extensive management capabilities for products, customers, and sales. Key features include:

Product and Customer Management: Users with the necessary authority can list new products, update or remove existing ones, manage customer profiles, configure shipping rates, and create/delete product categories and brands.

Role-Based Access Control: Admins can configure users with specific roles and permissions, granting tailored access to the platform's features. Admins can also manage email settings to enable notifications through the platform.

Real-Time Discount and Promotion Management: Sales personnel can post advertisements and apply discounts to products, which are reflected in real time across the platform.

Order and Shipping Updates: Sellers can view and manage customer orders, update the order status once products are packed and ready for dispatch, and rely on the integrated logistics API to automatically update order statuses after dispatch.

Sales Reporting: The platform offers detailed sales reports, allowing sellers to view data monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, and annually. This helps sellers gain valuable insights into their business performance and plan ahead effectively.

This backend platform ensures smooth e-commerce operations, providing real-time data updates and a comprehensive toolkit for managing products, customers, orders, and sales performance.
